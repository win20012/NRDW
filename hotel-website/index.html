Our Story

A Hidden Gem in the Old City

Welcome to NR&DW, an exclusive 7-bedroom boutique hotel perfectly situated within the ancient moat of Chiang Mai Old Town. We've crafted a space where traditional Lanna architecture meets contemporary comfort.

Step outside our doors and you are immediately immersed in the vibrant life of the city—a short walk will take you to legendary temples like Wat Chedi Luang, the famous Sunday Walking Street Market, and the absolute best of local cafes and dining.
